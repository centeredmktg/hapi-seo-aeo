# D2: Positioning and category

**The question:** what is Hapi, in words buyers already use, now that the product suite covers more than connectivity?

**Why it's first:** the category name goes into page titles, sales decks, analyst briefings, review-site categories and the investor story. Every downstream fix in the SEO plan (titles, Organization markup, hub pages) needs this answer first.

## Three layers, each with one job

| Layer | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | What we are, in words a buyer would type or say to their boss | Goes in titles and the first sentence of every page. Must be a term people already search. |
| **Thesis** | The bigger idea that explains why the category matters now | Headlines, decks, investor story. Can be new language. |
| **Proof** | What the suite does that others don't | Concrete and checkable. Products named by what they do. |

## Hypotheses to test

Each hypothesis says what would prove it wrong.

**H1. The category is "hospitality data platform."**
It covers all four products, the Lakehouse page already uses it, and buyers search it.
*Wrong if:* customers don't describe us this way in interviews, or Search Console shows buyers find us through a different term.

**H2. What sets Hapi apart is that it acts on the data, not just stores it.**
Most data platforms stop at "store and analyze." Hapi reads and writes, and Autopilot acts on its own (for example, loss prevention triggered by cancellations).
*Wrong if:* customers value Hapi mainly for connectivity breadth or identity resolution, and treat Autopilot as a side feature.

**H3. "Intelligence infrastructure" works as the thesis, not the category.**
In hotel tech, "intelligence" usually signals BI and revenue-management vendors, and nobody searches "intelligence infrastructure."
*Wrong if:* leadership has already committed to it, or buyers in interviews describe what Hapi gives them as "intelligence."

**H4. The connectivity heritage is a proof point, not the headline.**
Nine years of integration work is the reason the other three products are credible.
*Wrong if:* most new pipeline still starts with a connectivity need. Check this in HubSpot (D4).
