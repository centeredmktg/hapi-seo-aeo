# Positioning interview guide

**Who:** 5–12 customers across segments: a brand, a management company, an independent group, and at least one technology vendor partner. Add leadership separately, using the same opening questions.

**Length:** 20 minutes. The first three questions come before they hear any of our language.

**How to run it:** record with permission and capture exact words. Don't correct their vocabulary, because their vocabulary is the data.

## Before we say anything

1. If you had to explain Hapi to your boss in one sentence, what would you say?
2. What were you trying to fix when you started looking? What were you doing before Hapi?
3. What else did you look at, including building it yourselves?

## What they value

4. If Hapi went away tomorrow, what would break first?
5. Which Hapi capability would be hardest to replace?
6. Has anything happened automatically because of Hapi that used to take a person? Tell me about the last time.

## Words test (end of the call only)

7. Which of these sounds most like what Hapi is to you, and which sounds wrong?
   - connectivity partner
   - hospitality data platform
   - data infrastructure
   - integration platform
   - intelligence infrastructure
8. Is there a better word we're missing?
