146 of our 223 CMS pages publish with an empty page title and no meta description. Google writes its own titles for them, usually from a bare heading like "Mews." These include the IHG case study, the Nemacolin story and both Oracle OPERA pages. Hover a dot to see the page.

Why this matters beyond SEO

The 57 /connections pages are where a hotel or tech vendor checks whether we work with their systems. They're the closest thing we have to a storefront, and they are the weakest part of the site today:

45 of 57have no page title
~97%of each page is shared template text
36show "Data Streams: No items found"

Fixing these pages is SEO cleanup, and it's also the groundwork for any self-serve or partner-led motion. Someone who finds us through "[their PMS] integration" should land on a page that answers what flows, how fast, and who already runs it.

Our content already ranks, just not on our site. Copies of our press releases on Hospitality Net, Hotel-Online and Hotel Tech Report show up for four of six category searches we tested. hoteltech.review lists Hapi first among hotel API integration platforms, but doesn't link to us. Publishing on stayhapi.com first, then syndicating, captures rankings we've already earned.

What to fix, in order

Critical

Bind page titles and descriptions in the two CMS templates

101 resources and 45 connection pages ship with <title></title>. In Webflow's Collection template settings, bind SEO title, meta description and the social tags to CMS fields with a fallback to the item name.

Effort
About an hour per template
We'll know it worked
A re-run of the page check shows zero empty titles, and Search Console impressions for these pages rise within six weeks
Unblocks
Every other content and integration-page fix below
Critical

Publish the 2026 PMS API Study, and the other empty posts

About 18 resource pages are a headline and a date with no body, including the PMS API Study and the AI-Ready Data Framework. Every article links to the study through Related Posts, so a dead end is linked from every article. Original research is the asset most likely to earn links and AI citations, and a competitor's 2026 PMS API comparison is filling that slot today.

Effort
1–2 days for the study as an HTML page, gated PDF kept as the conversion
We'll know it worked
Impressions for "PMS API" searches and new referring domains within 60 days
High

Rebuild the connection page template

For each partner: what data flows each way, two or three use cases, customer proof, a link to the matching developer docs, and how setup works. Hide empty sections instead of showing "No items found." Start with the ten partners that have the most search demand and the most Hapi customers. Add an OPERA Cloud page, since searchers look for OPERA Cloud and Oracle's integration platform (OHIP), not OPERA 5.

High

Make stayhapi.com the original source for our news

Publish on our site first and syndicate three to seven days later. Ask syndication partners for a canonical link back where they allow it. Every release links to the matching product or connection page.

High

Merge duplicate pages and clean up leaked CMS slugs

The Revinate partnership exists four times. Nemacolin, IHG, Margaritaville, Rocco Forte and others exist twice, plus sevenrooms-2, shiji-2, and one slug ending in also-cover-on-website. Each copy competes with the original. Redirect each duplicate to one canonical page.

High

Tell search engines who Hapi is, in one name

On Bing, "Hapi" ranks us fifth, behind an investing app and the Egyptian god. Add Organization markup with our alternate names and profiles. Then settle the category name: the site currently calls us a connectivity partner, data infrastructure, a data-integrations platform, a cloud data hub and PMS middleware.

High

Bylines, article markup, alt text and page weight

47 posts have no author and one says "Author 1." Every image has empty alt text, including 130+ partner logos. The homepage loads 200 images with the hero set to load last, and one product page carries a 2.9MB GIF.

High

Confirm permissions on two reposted articles

Two third-party articles appear in full with the source field empty. Before changing anything, confirm what was agreed with the publishers, then add attribution and a canonical link, or unpublish.

Medium

Housekeeping

  • Placeholder text, "Bibendum amet at molestie mattis.", appears three times in the Connections menu on every page.
  • /product-overview links to a 404 and still sells Data Streams, Finders and Guest CRM.
  • A second, empty H1 appears on 164 of 166 articles, and "Related Posts" shows the same six posts everywhere.
  • 101 resources and 33 connection pages can only be reached through pagination.
  • Seven legal pages are in the sitemap.
  • Security headers beyond HSTS are missing, which hotel security reviews may ask about.
